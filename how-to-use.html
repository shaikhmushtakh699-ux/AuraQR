How to use AuraQR.

Create professional QR codes in a few simple steps. Choose your QR type, customize the design, generate your code, download it and share it anywhere.

1

Choose Your QR Type

Start by selecting the type of QR code you want to create. AuraQR supports different options such as URL, Text, Wi-Fi, WhatsApp, UPI Pay, Contact, Email, Phone, SMS, Location, Event and Socials.

Select a QR type and enter your information.

Tip: Select the QR type that matches the content you want to share.
Example: Use URL for a website and Wi-Fi for sharing Wi-Fi credentials.
2

Customize Your QR Design

Customize the appearance of your QR code. You can adjust colors, gradients and module styles to create a QR code that matches your brand or project.

Customize colors, gradients and module structure.

Tip: Keep strong contrast between the QR code and its background for better scanning.
3

Add Logo & Advanced Settings

Add a center logo to your QR code and adjust advanced options such as error correction, margin and render size.

Add your logo and adjust QR code settings.

Error Correction: Higher error correction can help a QR code remain scannable when a logo or other design elements are added.
4

Generate & Download

Once your QR code looks the way you want, generate it and choose the export format that fits your use case.

Generate QR Code

Click the Generate QR Code button to create your QR code.

Download PNG

Download a high-resolution PNG when you need a standard image format.

Export SVG

Use SVG when you need scalable vector artwork.

Print PDF

Use PDF when preparing QR codes for printing.

5

Scan, Test & Share

Before publishing or printing your QR code, scan it with a smartphone camera and confirm that it opens the correct destination.

📱 Scan

Open your phone camera and scan the QR code.

✓ Verify

Make sure the QR code opens the correct content.

📤 Share

Share your QR code digitally or use it in printed materials.

🖨️ Print

For physical use, test the printed QR code before distributing it.

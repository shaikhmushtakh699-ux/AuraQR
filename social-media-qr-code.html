What is a Social Media QR Code?

A Social Media QR Code is a QR code that contains a social media profile, channel or page URL. When someone scans the QR code, their device can open the linked social media page.

Supported Social Media Platforms

  • Instagram QR Code
  • YouTube QR Code
  • Facebook QR Code
  • X / Twitter QR Code
  • LinkedIn QR Code
  • Telegram QR Code
  • Other social media profile URLs

How to Create a Social Media QR Code

  • Select your social media platform.
  • Enter your profile or channel name.
  • Paste your social media profile URL.
  • Click Generate Social Media QR Code.
  • Test the QR code with a smartphone.
  • Download the QR code as a PNG image.

Where Can You Use Social Media QR Codes?

Social media QR codes can be used on business cards, posters, flyers, product packaging, restaurant menus, storefronts, events, advertisements, presentations, YouTube videos and other marketing materials.

Social Media QR Codes for Business

Businesses can use QR codes to make their social media profiles easier to discover. Customers can scan a printed QR code and open the linked profile without manually typing a username or web address.

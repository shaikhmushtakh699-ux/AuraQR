Why Use QR Code Frames?

QR code frames help make QR codes more noticeable and easier to understand. Add a simple call-to-action such as "SCAN ME", "VIEW MENU", "VISIT US" or "GET STARTED". AuraQR creates the QR code directly in your browser and lets you download the result.

QR Code Frames FAQ

What are QR code frames?

QR code frames are decorative designs placed around a QR code, often with a call-to-action message that encourages people to scan.

Can I customize the frame color?

Yes. You can choose a custom frame color and text color using the color controls on this page.

Can I download the framed QR code?

Yes. Generate your QR code and use the Download PNG button to save the QR code as a PNG image.

Does the QR code expire?

The QR code generated here is a standard static QR code. It does not expire as long as the information encoded in it remains valid.

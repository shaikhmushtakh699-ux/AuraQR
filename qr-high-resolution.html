High Resolution QR Code FAQ

What is a high resolution QR code?

A high-resolution QR code is generated at a larger pixel size so it can remain sharp when used in larger digital or printed designs.

What resolution should I use for printing?

The appropriate resolution depends on the physical print size and scanning distance. For larger printed materials, a higher-resolution QR code can provide more flexibility.

Can I download the QR code in high quality?

Yes. AuraQR generates the QR code locally in your browser and allows you to download the generated PNG at the selected resolution.

Will a high-resolution QR code scan better?

Resolution alone does not guarantee better scanning. Contrast, quiet zone, physical size and correct QR code generation are also important.

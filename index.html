Frequently Asked Questions
Are these QR codes permanent and do they expire? +

All QR codes created on AuraQR are standard static QR codes. They never expire, have zero scan limits, and function indefinitely without ongoing subscriptions.

Will my QR code remain scannable after adding a logo? +

Yes. AuraQR automatically raises error correction to Level Q or H (up to 30% recovery capability) to ensure scanner sensors can read data around the center logo reliably.

Can I use downloaded QR codes for commercial projects? +

Yes. All generated assets are 100% yours to use commercially across merchandise, restaurant menus, packaging, and digital advertisements.

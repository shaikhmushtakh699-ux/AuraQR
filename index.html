Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
What is a QR Code? +

A QR (Quick Response) code is a machine-readable optical barcode containing data that can be quickly scanned by smart devices and camera apps to direct users to links, contacts, or payments.

How do I create a QR Code? +

Simply choose your data type (e.g. Website URL), enter your text or URL, customize the colors and module shapes, and click download to get your high-resolution image.

Is AuraQR free? +

Yes, AuraQR is 100% free forever. There are no scan caps, trial periods, or hidden subscriptions.

Do I need to create an account? +

No account or registration is required. You can generate unlimited custom QR codes instantly upon opening the page.

What QR Code types are supported? +

AuraQR supports 12 dynamic types: Website URLs, Plain Text, Wi-Fi credentials, WhatsApp direct chat, UPI payment links, vCard contacts, Email, Phone dialing, SMS, Map Locations, Calendar Events, and Social Media profiles.

Can I add a logo to my QR code? +

Yes! You can upload any image icon (PNG, JPG, SVG) to center inside your QR code. AuraQR automatically increases error correction to preserve scan fidelity.

Will my QR Code expire? +

No. All QR codes created on AuraQR are standard static codes. They store raw data directly inside the matrix, meaning they work permanently without expiring.

Which download formats are supported? +

You can export QR codes in high-definition PNG, standard JPG, print PDF, and pure scalable Vector SVG format.

Why is my QR Code not scanning? +

Ensure there is sufficient color contrast between the primary modules and the background. If using a logo, increase the Error Correction level to Q (25%) or H (30%).

Is my data stored on any server? +

No. All barcode generation logic is performed 100% locally in your browser. Your sensitive payload data is never transmitted to an external server.

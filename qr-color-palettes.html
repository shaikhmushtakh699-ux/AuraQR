QR Code Color FAQ

Can I use colors in a QR code?

Yes. QR codes can use colors, but the foreground and background should have strong enough contrast for reliable scanning.

What is the best color for a QR code?

Dark foreground colors combined with a light background generally provide good readability and scanning reliability.

Can I create a branded QR code?

Yes. AuraQR supports customized QR code designs, including colors and QR codes with logos.

Can I use these palettes for business QR codes?

Yes. Professional palettes can be useful for business cards, menus, packaging, posters, marketing materials and branded QR codes.

Should a QR code have high contrast?

Yes. High contrast between the QR modules and background is important for reliable scanning.

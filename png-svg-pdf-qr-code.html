Frequently Asked Questions

Can I download a QR code as PNG?

Yes. AuraQR lets you download your generated QR code as a PNG image.

What is an SVG QR code?

SVG is a vector format that can be scaled to different sizes without losing sharpness. It is especially useful for professional design and printing.

Can I create a PDF QR code?

Yes. AuraQR can place your generated QR code into a downloadable PDF file.

Which format is best for printing?

SVG is generally a strong choice when you need a scalable QR graphic for professional design and printing. PDF is also useful for print documents.

Are my QR codes stored online?

QR generation on this page happens in your browser. Your entered content is used locally to create the QR code.

Is AuraQR free?

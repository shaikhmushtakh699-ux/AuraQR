App QR Code FAQ

What is an App QR code?

An App QR code is a QR code containing a link to a mobile application download page. Users can scan it to open the app link on their device.

Can I create an Android App QR code?

Yes. You can enter your Google Play Store application URL and generate a QR code for it.

Can I create an iPhone App QR code?

Yes. Enter your Apple App Store application URL and generate the QR code.

Is the App QR code free?

Yes. AuraQR provides this App QR code generator free of charge.

Can I download the App QR code?

Yes. After generating the QR code, click Download QR Code to save it as a PNG image.

Will the App QR code expire?

The QR code itself does not expire. However, the QR code will only work while the app URL you provided remains accessible.

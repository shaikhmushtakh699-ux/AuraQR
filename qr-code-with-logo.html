QR Code with Logo FAQ

Common questions about creating QR codes with logos.

Can I create a QR code with my own logo?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG or WEBP logo and AuraQR places it in the center of the generated QR code.

What logo size should I use?

A logo around 15% to 25% of the QR code is generally a good starting point. Always test the final QR code with a phone before publishing it.

Which error correction should I use with a logo?

High or Highest error correction is recommended when placing a logo over the QR code because QR codes with higher correction can tolerate more damage or obstruction.

Is the QR code free?

Yes. You can create and download the QR code using AuraQR without paying for QR generation.

Does a logo QR code expire?

A static QR code itself does not expire. If it contains a URL, the destination website must continue to work.

Can I use the QR code for business?

Yes. Logo QR codes can be useful for business cards, menus, packaging, posters, product labels, advertisements and marketing materials.

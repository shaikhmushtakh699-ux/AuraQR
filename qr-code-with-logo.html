Free QR Code with Logo Generator

AuraQR lets you create customized QR codes with your own brand logo. Logo QR codes are useful for businesses, marketing campaigns, websites, menus, posters, packaging and business cards.

For reliable scanning, use a clear logo and keep enough contrast between the QR code and its background.

How to Create a QR Code with Logo

1. Enter the website URL you want to encode.
2. Upload your logo image.
3. Click Generate QR Code.
4. Test the QR code with a phone camera.
5. Download the QR code as a PNG image.

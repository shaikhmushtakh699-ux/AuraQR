UPI QR Code FAQ

What is a UPI QR code?

A UPI QR code contains UPI payment information that compatible payment applications can read to start a payment.

Can I create a UPI QR code for free?

Yes. AuraQR provides this UPI QR code generator directly in your browser.

Can I add a fixed amount?

Yes. You can optionally enter an amount. If you leave the amount field empty, the payer can enter the amount in their payment app, depending on the app's supported behavior.

Does AuraQR process my payment?

No. AuraQR only generates the QR code. The actual payment is handled by your UPI-compatible payment application and payment provider.

Is my UPI ID uploaded to a server?

This page generates the QR code in your browser. AuraQR does not need to upload your UPI ID to a server to generate the QR code.

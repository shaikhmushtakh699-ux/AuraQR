Free QR Code Design Generator

AuraQR helps you create a customized QR code for websites, businesses, marketing campaigns, posters, flyers, packaging, menus and social media.

Choose your QR code color, background color and QR size. A good contrast between the QR code and its background helps smartphones scan the code more reliably.

How to Design a QR Code

1. Enter the URL you want to encode.
2. Choose your QR code color.
3. Choose your background color.
4. Select the QR code size.
5. Click Generate Designed QR Code.
6. Test the QR code with a smartphone.
7. Download the finished QR code.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize my QR code?

Yes. You can customize the QR code color, background color and size.

What colors can I use?

You can choose custom colors using the built-in color selectors.

What QR code size should I choose?

Choose a larger size when the QR code will be printed or displayed from a greater distance.

Are custom QR codes scannable?

Yes, as long as the QR code maintains sufficient contrast and clear scanning areas. Always test the final QR code before publishing it.

Can I download my designed QR code?

Yes. You can download the generated QR code as a PNG image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I change the QR code color?

Yes. Use the QR Code Color selector to choose a custom color. For reliable scanning, maintain strong contrast between the QR code and its background.

Can I change the background color?

Yes. AuraQR lets you select a custom background color. You can also enable the transparent background option.

What is QR error correction?

Error correction allows a QR code to remain readable even when part of its data pattern is damaged or obscured. Higher levels provide more recovery capability.

Are these QR codes static?

Yes. The QR code contains the content you enter when it is generated. If you change the destination later, you need to create a new QR code.

Is my entered data uploaded?

QR generation on this page happens directly in your browser. The page does not need to upload the entered text to AuraQR's server to create the QR code.

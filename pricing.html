Pricing FAQ

Can I use AuraQR for free?

Yes. AuraQR provides a Free plan for creating standard static QR codes.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can start with Free and upgrade to Pro or Business whenever you need additional features.

Will my QR codes expire?

Static QR codes generated by AuraQR do not expire as long as their underlying information remains valid.

What is the difference between Pro and Business?

Pro is designed for advanced individual use and customization. Business is intended for higher-volume workflows and advanced business features.

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AuraQR handles information and technologies that may be used when visitors access and use the website.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. About AuraQR

AuraQR is an online QR code generation service available at auraqr.in. The website provides tools for creating QR codes for different types of information, including URLs, text, Wi-Fi, contacts, email, phone numbers, locations, events, social links and other supported formats.

2. Information You Enter

Information entered into the QR generator is used to create the QR code requested by the user. Depending on the QR type, this may include a website URL, text, contact information, Wi-Fi details, payment information or other content supplied by the user.

Users should avoid entering sensitive or confidential information into a QR code unless doing so is necessary for their intended use.

3. QR Code Generation

AuraQR's QR generation features are designed to perform generation within the user's browser where supported by the website's implementation. Generated QR information may also be stored locally in the browser when a feature such as QR history is used.

Local browser storage is controlled by the user's browser and can generally be cleared through browser settings.

4. Contact Information

If you voluntarily contact AuraQR or provide information through a contact feature, the information you provide may include your name, email address and the contents of your message.

Do not submit passwords, payment credentials or other highly sensitive information through a general contact form.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

AuraQR and third-party services used on the website may use cookies, local storage or similar technologies for functionality, preferences, analytics and advertising purposes.

Your browser may allow you to control or delete cookies and stored website data. Some website features may behave differently when these technologies are disabled.

6. Analytics

AuraQR may use analytics technologies to understand general website usage, such as page visits, traffic sources and interaction with the website. Analytics information helps us understand how visitors use the service and where the website can be improved.

7. Advertising

AuraQR may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure and personalize advertising according to their applicable policies and user settings.

Users can review Google's advertising and privacy information through Google's own services and settings.

8. Third-Party Services

AuraQR may use third-party services for website hosting, analytics, advertising, authentication, payments or other functionality as the website develops.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of the website and its associated systems. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

AuraQR is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. Visitors should use the website in accordance with applicable laws and with appropriate parental or guardian guidance where required.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AuraQR introduces new features, services or technologies. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

12. Contact AuraQR

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of information on AuraQR, you can contact us through the contact section of the website.

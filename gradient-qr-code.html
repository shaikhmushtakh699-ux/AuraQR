Frequently Asked Questions

What is a gradient QR code?

A gradient QR code uses a color transition between two or more colors instead of a single solid color.

Can I choose my own QR colors?

Yes. You can choose both the starting and ending colors using the color selectors.

Are gradient QR codes scannable?

They can be scanned when there is sufficient contrast between the QR code and its background. Always test the final QR code before use.

Can I download the gradient QR code?

Yes. After generating the QR code, click the Download QR Code button to save it as a PNG image.

Can I use a gradient QR code for business?

Yes. Gradient QR codes can be used for branding, marketing campaigns, printed materials, packaging and business promotions.

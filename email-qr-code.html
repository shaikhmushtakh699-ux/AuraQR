Email QR Code FAQ

What is an email QR code?

An email QR code stores a mailto link that can open an email application with the recipient and optional message information.

Is the email QR code free?

Yes. AuraQR provides free QR code generation directly in your browser.

Can I add a subject and message?

Yes. You can add both an email subject and a pre-filled message.

Does AuraQR store my email address?

QR generation happens directly in your browser. The email information is used to create the QR code on the page.
